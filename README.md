### About

Biology generates some of the most complex, high-dimensional data in science — and I find that genuinely exciting to work with. Not just running tools, but actually understanding what the data is saying: which cells are behaving differently, which variants matter, which genes are driving survival outcomes.

I'm drawn to the problems that sit at the intersection of biology and computation — where the right analysis pipeline can turn noise into a discovery. That's what I build.

- 🎓 MS Bioinformatics — Northeastern University (Dec 2025)
- 🔬 scRNA-seq · Bulk RNA-seq · Somatic Variant Analysis · Survival Biomarker Discovery
- 🤖 In progress: AMR prediction via multi-omics deep learning + SHAP explainability
- 📍 Boston, MA — open to roles across the US
- 🔗 [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pankti-p-9841b523b)
